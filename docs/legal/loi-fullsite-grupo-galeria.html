Letter of Intent
Fullsite × Grupo Galeria
This Letter of Intent ("LOI") confirms the mutual intent of Monica Garcia Pons, in her capacity as a Board Member of Grupo Galeria, and Daniel Ramonfaur, in his capacity as Founder & CEO of Fullsite, to explore the deployment of Fullsite's Restaurant Operating System within Grupo Galeria's restaurant brands: Dunkin Mexico, Carl's Jr, BWW, IHOP.
1. Intent
Grupo Galeria will evaluate Fullsite's platform — including POS, Kitchen Display System, Inventory, Purchasing, Dashboards, and AI Intelligence Layer — through pilots in selected locations, with the possibility of expanding to additional brands and formats if the pilots are successful.
2. Non-Binding Nature
This LOI is not a binding agreement. Neither party is obligated to proceed with a commercial relationship unless and until a definitive agreement is signed.
3. Next Steps
Both parties will work in good faith to define pilot parameters, including location selection, timeline, and success metrics, within the next 6 months.
