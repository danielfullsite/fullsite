Operational Intelligence

36 analytical agents. Not chatbots — autonomous operational workflows.

Purchase Predictor

Every Monday: analyzes 30-day purchase history, explodes recipes into ingredient demand, projects 7-day need with 15% safety margin. Grouped by department and supplier. Operator reviews and approves.

Anomaly Detector

Compares real-time sales against historical day-of-week patterns. Detects when a metric deviates significantly — before the owner notices.

Climate + Events

Fetches 3-day weather forecast, cross-references with 90 days of sales history and 40+ local events (holidays, paydays, World Cup). Recommends menu adjustments.

Also: fraud detection, waste analysis, menu engineering, staffing optimization, table rotation, speed of service, upselling, supplier monitoring, cost variance, CRM.
